🛰️ GPS Setup Information:

ROVER (Front GPS): This GPS is at the front of your vessel and determines direction

BASE (Back GPS): This GPS is at the back of your vessel and provides reference

Direction: System calculates heading from BASE → ROVER

💡 How to find COM ports:
1. Press Windows + X → "Device Manager"
2. Expand "Ports (COM & LPT)"
3. Look for "USB Serial Port (COM7)" or "u-blox"
4. Note the COM port numbers (e.g., COM7, COM12)
